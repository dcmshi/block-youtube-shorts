# Privacy Policy

Shorts Blocker for YouTube does not collect, send or sell any data.

- Your on/off settings are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`. That means Chrome or Firefox syncs them between your own browsers if you have sync turned on.
- The extension makes no network requests and has no analytics or tracking.
- It only runs on `youtube.com` pages, where it hides page elements and rewrites Shorts URLs.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
